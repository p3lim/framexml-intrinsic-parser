This is an [[intrinsic frame]]. It has all the methods from {{api|t=o|Button}} and these mixins:
* {{tlygo|AuraButtonInboundMixin}}
* {{tlygo|AuraButtonSharedMixin}}

If {{tlygo|CustomAuraButtonTemplate}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|CustomAuraButtonInboundMixin}}
* {{tlygo|CustomAuraButtonSharedMixin}}
