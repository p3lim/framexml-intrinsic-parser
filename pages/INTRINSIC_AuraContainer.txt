This is an [[intrinsic frame]]. It has all the methods from {{api|t=o|Frame}} and these mixins:
* {{tlygo|AuraContainerInboundMixin}}
* {{tlygo|AuraContainerSharedMixin}}

If {{tlygo|CustomAuraContainerTemplate}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|AuraContainerFlowLayoutInboundMixin}}
* {{tlygo|AuraContainerFlowLayoutSharedMixin}}
* {{tlygo|AuraContainerInboundMixin}}
* {{tlygo|AuraContainerSharedMixin}}
* {{tlygo|CustomAuraContainerInboundMixin}}
* {{tlygo|CustomAuraContainerSharedMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ManagedAuraContainerInboundMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ManagedAuraContainerSharedMixin}}
