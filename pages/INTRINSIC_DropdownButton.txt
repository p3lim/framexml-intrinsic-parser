This is an [[intrinsic frame]]. It has all the methods from {{api|t=o|Button}} and these mixins:
* {{tlygo|CallbackRegistryMixin}}
* {{tlygo|DropdownButtonMixin}}

If {{tlygo|UIPanelIconDropdownButtonTemplate}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|UIPanelIconDropdownButtonMixin}}
If {{tlygo|WowStyle2DropdownTemplate}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|ButtonStateBehaviorMixin}}
* {{tlygo|DropdownSelectionTextMixin}}
* {{tlygo|DropdownTextMixin}}
* {{tlygo|WowDropdownFilterBehaviorMixin}}
* {{tlygo|WowFilterButtonMixin}}
* {{tlygo|WowStyle2DropdownMixin}}
If {{tlygo|WowStyle1DropdownTemplate}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|ButtonStateBehaviorMixin}}
* {{tlygo|DropdownSelectionTextMixin}}
* {{tlygo|DropdownTextMixin}}
* {{tlygo|WowStyle1DropdownMixin}}
If {{tlygo|WowStyle1ArrowDropdownTemplate}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|ButtonStateBehaviorMixin}}
* {{tlygo|WowStyle1ArrowDropdownMixin}}
If {{tlygo|WowStyle1FilterDropdownTemplate}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|ButtonStateBehaviorMixin}}
* {{tlygo|DropdownTextMixin}}
* {{tlygo|WowDropdownFilterBehaviorMixin}}
* {{tlygo|WowFilterButtonMixin}}
* {{tlygo|WowStyle1FilterDropdownMixin}}
