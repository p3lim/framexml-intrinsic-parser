This is an [[intrinsic frame]]. It has all the methods from {{api|t=o|Frame}} and these mixins:
* {{tlygo|CallbackRegistryMixin}}
* {{tlygo|EventFrameMixin}}

If {{tlygo|ScrollBarBaseTemplate}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|CallbackRegistryMixin}}
* {{tlygo|EventFrameMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ScrollBarMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ScrollControllerMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ScrollDirectionMixin}}
If {{tlygo|VerticalScrollBarTemplate}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|CallbackRegistryMixin}}
* {{tlygo|EventFrameMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ScrollBarMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ScrollControllerMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ScrollDirectionMixin}}
If {{tlygo|HorizontalScrollBarTemplate}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|CallbackRegistryMixin}}
* {{tlygo|EventFrameMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ScrollBarMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ScrollControllerMixin}}
* {{tlygo|ScrollDirectionMixin}}
If {{tlygo|WowTrimScrollBar}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|WowTrimScrollBarMixin}}
If {{tlygo|WowTrimHorizontalScrollBar}} is inherited the methods from these mixins are also available:
* {{tlygo|WowTrimScrollBarMixin}}
